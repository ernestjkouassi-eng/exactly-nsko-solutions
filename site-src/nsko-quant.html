02 · How it works

How powerful it is

A research team, a strict test lab, a ten-step trade flow and a risk system that cannot be switched off in live trading.

Research

The JARVIS research team

A team of research agents reads YouTube, GitHub, arXiv papers and news feeds. They summarise what they find, check the trading terms and turn good ideas into strategy recipes for the Lab. You can watch them work in the Agent Room.

The Lab

Honest testing, before any money

Every recipe is tested as ten variants. Walk-forward testing trains on older data and judges on newer data the strategy has never seen.

  • Pass mark: Sharpe 1.40 out of sample, after trading costs
  • At least 30 trades and no more than 20% drawdown
  • Overfitting checks, Monte Carlo, and it must beat random
  • Then 21 days and 30 trades on paper before live can unlock

The trade flow

Ten steps. Every trade. Every time.

Each step can stop a trade. None can skip the risk gate. You can follow each one live on the Trade Card.

  1. ScannerApproved strategies only, on closed bars only.
  2. News blackoutNo new trades 15 minutes either side of a high-impact event.
  3. Forecast1,000 possible price paths. At least 55% must agree.
  4. EntrySignal from a closed bar, entry at the live quote.
  5. Time Machine10,000 replays. Expected value after costs must be above zero.
  6. SizingSize from your maximum loss and the stop distance, rounded down.
  7. Risk gateHard limits on every trade. They cannot be switched off in live.
  8. DebateAnalyst, bull, bear, risk and trader. It can veto a trade, never force one.
  9. Bracket orderStop and target placed at the broker. Half off at 1R, stop to breakeven.
  10. Exit and journalEvery step logged to the audit trail, with an alert on your phone.

God's Eye

World events, on a globe

God's Eye follows flights, ships, satellites, earthquakes, fires, weather, launches and crop belts, and flags events that can move a market. It never opens a trade. If you choose, it can only reduce risk: pause new entries or cut size.

The Alt-Data Lab adds Crop Watch from Sentinel-2 satellite scenes and Oil Storage from public energy data.

03 · Engineering

How we built it

Tauri desktop shell

A native app with a tray icon that shows the engine's state, and pause and kill controls that work with the window closed.

Python engine

Runs on your computer and talks to your computer only. It refuses to start on any other network address.

159 automated tests

Every risk block, the kill switch, bracket orders, reboot recovery and a no-look-ahead check, plus a 53-step end-to-end run.

Tamper-proof audit log

Append-only and hash-chained. The database refuses edits and deletes, and any change breaks the chain.

Offline Ed25519 licences

Your licence is signed by NSKO and checked on your computer, with no server call.

Keys in the OS keychain

Keychain on Mac, Credential Manager on Windows. The app can save a key but never read it back. Never in backups or logs.

07 · Questions

FAQ

Does NSKO hold my money?

No. Your money stays in your own broker account. NSKO QUANT connects with trade-only API keys, with withdrawals switched off, and the keys stay in your computer's keychain. The app has no withdrawal function: its withdraw helper only shows what is free to withdraw and links to your broker's own page.

Which brokers?

Ready: Paper, Alpaca and Kraken. Built: Coinbase and Interactive Brokers. In development: Capital.com, IG, OANDA and MT5. CMC Markets is not supported, because it has no retail API for automated trading.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes. The engine runs on your computer, so it trades only while the computer is on and online. It runs as a background service, keeps the computer awake and restarts itself if it gets stuck. After a restart it checks your open positions and puts back any missing stop. Stops sit at the broker, so open trades keep their stop even if your computer goes off.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Trading carries real risk and any strategy can lose. The risk limits, paper trading and the go-live checklist reduce risk; they do not remove it. Past and simulated results do not guarantee future results. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

Is it financial advice?

No. NSKO QUANT is software. You choose the strategies, the risk limits and when to go live. We do not know your circumstances, so speak to a regulated financial adviser if you are unsure.
