# Style guide from the 3 reference videos (grammar only, no content reused)

Contact sheets: `ref/ref_01_sheet.jpg`, `ref/ref_02_sheet.jpg`, `ref/ref_03_sheet.jpg` (one frame every 0.5 s).

## ref_01 (30 s, chain on a grey velvet bust)
- One continuous slow handheld orbit: wide on the bust → slides down the drape → **macro push onto the clasp (about 6 to 15 s)** → pulls back and travels along the chain → returns wide. No cuts.
- Shallow depth of field, focus follows the clasp. Background: dark showroom, soft practical lights, out of focus.
- Light: big soft top light on the velvet, many small specular points on stones (that is where the sparkle comes from).
- Take: the orbit, the macro push onto the clasp, the drape shape. Leave out the person, the room and the TikTok marks.

## ref_02 (12 s, black background macro)
- Pure black velvet set. Extreme macro slides across stones, slow, then a **speed ramp** (fast whip) into the next angle.
- Hard small lights give crisp fire; blacks stay black. Cool neutral grade.
- Take: the black set, the macro slide, the speed ramp as a transition between pieces.

## ref_03 (12 s, case reveal)
- Lit dark set, piece revealed in a case, then a hero turntable of the pendant on a black bust.
- Take the **pacing**: a held beat before the reveal, then the hero turn. Ignore the presenter, captions and the teal case.

## Our grammar
- Palette: deep neutral black #050506, velvet charcoal #141416, grey velvet bust #C9C4C0, rose gold #F2B8A0, platinum #E3E5E8, canary #E9C24A, sapphire #1D3E8C. White highlights. No teal-orange.
- Camera: 100 mm macro look, f/2.8 to f/5.6, slow ease-in-out moves only, focus pulls only during real motion. Transitions = continuous moves or a short speed ramp, never a graphic wipe.
- Type: thin geometric sans, uppercase, letterspaced (labels 24 to 28 px at 3K). Title: 3D brushed rose-gold letters lit by a moving light.
- Motion: only real camera and object motion. No sparkle stickers, no flares, no glow overlays, no bounce.
