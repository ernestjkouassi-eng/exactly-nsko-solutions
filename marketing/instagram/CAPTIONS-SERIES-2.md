# NSKO Solutions · Instagram series 2: "switch it up"

The first 10 posts were brand statements. These 12 give people a reason to **stop, play, swipe, comment and save**.
Files: `series2/post-NN.jpg`. A name like `post-11-1.jpg` means post 11, slide 1 (a carousel). Upload the slides in order.
Honesty rules still apply: no made-up clients or numbers; concept visuals and the diary say so on the image.
