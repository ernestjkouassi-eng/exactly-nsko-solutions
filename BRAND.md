# NSKO Solutions brand

Source of truth: `brand/tokens.json`. Logo files: `brand/logo/`.

## Logo: the exploded N
Three black bars and one orange fragment broken off the top of the diagonal, on a 100x100 grid:
left stem `rect x0 y0 w22 h100` · fragment `polygon 26,0 56,0 58.88,16 28.88,16` (orange) · diagonal `polygon 29.96,22 59.96,22 74,100 44,100` · right stem `rect x78 y0 w22 h100`.
Wordmark: NSKO in Inter Black, SOLUTIONS letterspaced underneath. On dark, bars go white; the fragment stays orange.
Files: `nsko-symbol(.svg|-white.svg)`, `nsko-solutions-lockup(.svg|-white.svg)`, `nsko-app-icon.svg`. The wordmark is outlined (paths), so it renders the same everywhere.

## Type
Headlines: Inter Black, capitals, tracking -4%. Body: Inter Regular. Labels and data: JetBrains Mono, uppercase, letterspaced.

## Words
Slogan: **WE BUILD WHAT'S NEXT.** · CTA: **Bring us the hard problem.** · Sign-off: **Built by NSKO.** · Tenders: **Engineered in Britain.**
Plain British English. No fake numbers, clients, years or certifications. Concepts say "Concept". Never "Coming soon".

## Modes
Light by default with a dark switch. The hero, Machine Lab and NSKO Secure stay dark in both.
