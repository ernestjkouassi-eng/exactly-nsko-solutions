01 · What's included

What you get

Three parts that work together on your computer, plus your broker, which you choose.

1 · The desktop app

The screens you look at: charts, the Lab, the Trade Card, Settings and more. Windows, Mac and Linux. Closing the window hides it to the tray. Trading carries on.

2 · The engine

A background program that does the trading. It runs 24/7 as long as the computer is on and online. It talks only to the app on the same computer.

3 · The paper account

A built-in practice account with pretend money (in £). Every new desk starts here. You cannot skip it.

Also inside

  • A research team of software "agents" that reads trading ideas and turns them into rules to test.
  • The Lab, which tests every idea against a strict pass mark, after costs.
  • A hard risk gate. It cannot be switched off when you trade live.
  • A kill switch in the app, in the tray and on Telegram.
  • Phone alerts through ntfy and Telegram.
  • God's Eye: a world map of events (flights, quakes, weather) for context only.
  • A Withdraw helper that shows what is free to take out. It never moves money.
  • Export / Restore my desk: an encrypted backup (never your broker keys).

What NSKO QUANT does not do

  • It does not hold your money. Your money stays with your broker, in your name.
  • It cannot withdraw money. There is no withdrawal code in the engine at all.
  • It does not give financial advice, and it cannot promise a profit.
  • It does not send your account, password or keys to NSKO. Your login lives on your computer only. There is no NSKO login server.
02 · What it does

Every trade walks the same steps

The engine checks a trade idea step by step. Any step can say no. A "no" means the trade is skipped and the reason is written down.

Stops always sit at the broker

The stop order is placed with your broker, not only on your computer. So if your computer or internet goes down, the stop still protects the trade. If a stop cannot be placed and checked after 3 tries, the engine closes the position.

Before anything trades, an idea has to earn its place. This is the research pipeline:

Who decides

You do. A strategy only reaches paper trading when you press To paper. It only reaches live when you have finished the live checklist (section 8).

What "Sharpe" means

A score for how smooth returns were compared with their ups and downs. Higher is better. NSKO QUANT asks for at least 1.40 on data the strategy never saw, after trading costs.

Download the installer for your computer from NSKO Solutions, install it, then follow the 7-step welcome.

The lock screen always has a Use demo data instead button. It shows the built-in simulator, so you can explore safely. Demo screens are marked DEMO DATA in the top bar.

05 · Keeping it running 24/7

Keep the engine awake

The engine is a separate program from the window. Closing the window does not stop trading. Turning the computer off does.

5.1The tray icon

NSKO QUANT puts an icon in the tray (Windows, bottom right) or menu bar (Mac, top right). Its dot shows the state:

  • Green: trading.
  • Amber: paused (no new trades).
  • Red: a problem: the engine is not answering, or the kill switch is on.

Click it for the menu: Open NSKO QUANT, Pause / Resume new trades, Kill switch… and Quit engine…. The last two ask you to confirm first.

ILLUSTRATIVE drawing of the tray menu. The look depends on your system.

5.2Start on boot and keep-awake

  • Start on boot. Welcome step 3 turns this on. You can change it in Settings → Desktop: "Start NSKO QUANT when the computer turns on (window hidden, tray icon only)".
  • Keep-awake. While the engine runs it asks the computer not to sleep. The screen can still switch off; that is fine. The Engine screen shows KEEP-AWAKE: ON.
  • Engine as a service (best for 24/7). The engine can be installed as a background service, so it starts with the computer and restarts if it stops. NSKO support can set this up with you.

5.3Power settings

Windows
  1. Open Settings → System → Power (on some versions Power & battery).
  2. Set "When plugged in, put my device to sleep after" to Never. The screen can still turn off.
  3. Laptops: in Control Panel → Power Options → "Choose what closing the lid does", set When I close the lid (plugged in) to Do nothing.
  4. Turn off automatic restarts for updates at times you are trading, or set "active hours".

Menu names may differ between Windows versions.

Mac
  1. Open System Settings → Energy (or Battery → Options on a laptop).
  2. Turn on "Prevent automatic sleeping when the display is off" (wording varies).
  3. Laptops: keep it plugged in. Closing the lid normally puts a Mac to sleep unless an external display is connected, so leave the lid open.
  4. Turn on "Start up automatically after a power failure" if your Mac offers it.

Menu names may differ between macOS versions.

5.4What happens if…

EventWhat NSKO QUANT does
The internet dropsNo new trades open. Nothing is cancelled: your stops and targets are already at the broker and keep protecting you. The engine retries, and checks everything with the broker when the connection is back.
The computer restartsWith start on boot or the service, the engine comes back by itself. It asks the broker what is open. The broker is the source of truth. Any open position missing a stop or target gets them put back. If a stop still cannot be placed, the position is closed.
The engine gets stuckA watchdog checks every 15 seconds. If the engine has not answered for 60 seconds, it is restarted.
The window is closedNothing changes. The window hides to the tray. Use Quit engine… in the tray menu to really stop.
The app cannot reach the engineThe screens switch to demo data with a notice, and keep retrying. The tray dot turns red.

5.5Optional: an always-on server

If you cannot keep a computer on, the engine can also run on a small always-on machine (for example a home mini-PC or a rented server) using Docker. On a server the engine only listens on that machine itself, never on the open internet, and your keys are protected by a passphrase you set. This route is more technical: ask NSKO support before you try it.

Good habit

Once a week, open the Engine screen and check: status running, keep-awake on, last heartbeat a few seconds ago.

06 · Connect your broker

Your money stays with your broker

NSKO QUANT only needs a key that can place trades. You switch withdrawals OFF on that key. Then even if the key were stolen, it could not move your money out.

6.1The same six steps for every broker

  1. Open the account. Do it on the broker's own website. Use a demo or paper account first if the broker has one.
  2. Create an API key. An API key is a password for software. Brokers usually keep this under Settings, Security or API.
  3. Set the permissions: trading ON, withdrawals OFF. Tick only what is needed to view your account and place orders. Never tick withdraw or transfer.
  4. Paste it into NSKO QUANT. Go to Brokers, press Connect on your broker, choose Demo account or Live account, paste the key (and secret, if there is one), then press Save key and connect.
  5. Test. The box shows "Connected · trading ON · withdrawals OFF ✓", or explains what is wrong.
  6. Demo first. Run on paper or the broker's demo account before any real money. Live stays locked until the checklist in section 8 is done.
Where your key goes

The key is saved in your computer's own secure keychain (Keychain on Mac, Credential Manager on Windows). The window can save a key but can never read it back. Keys are never in backups and never in logs.

Broker menus change from time to time. The steps on the next pages are generic: menu names may differ.

10 · Auto vs Manual · Kill switch · Phone alerts

Stay in control

10.1Auto or Manual

Auto

Every step runs by itself. If all checks pass, the order is sent. You get an alert.

Manual

Every check still runs. At the Execute step the engine waits for you. Press Approve on the Trade Card or in Decisions within 60 seconds. If you do nothing, the trade is skipped.

Switch in Settings → Trading, or with the Auto / Manual buttons on the Trade Card.

10.2The kill switch: three ways

The kill switch cancels every open order, closes every open position and locks trading until you turn it back on.

In the app

Press and hold KILL SWITCH for 1.5 seconds (top right). Holding stops accidental clicks.

In the tray

Tray icon → Kill switch… → confirm. Works with the window closed.

From your phone

Send /kill to your NSKO QUANT Telegram bot. Only your paired chat is obeyed; others are refused and logged.

If the kill switch reports a problem

If the broker refuses a cancel or close (for example because it is offline), the app shows a red alert that stays on screen. Then log in at your broker's website and close the positions there yourself.

10.3Phone alerts

NSKO QUANT sends an alert for every trade, every error, the kill switch and a daily summary. Trades and errors go out as high priority; the kill switch goes out as urgent.

ntfy (free)
  1. Install the ntfy app on your phone.
  2. Subscribe to a topic name that is long and hard to guess, like nsq-desk-7f3k9q2. Anyone who knows the name can read it.
  3. Give the same topic name to NSKO QUANT (the ntfy topic setting). The server is ntfy.sh unless you run your own.
  4. Allow ntfy to make sound for high priority messages.
Telegram (free)
  1. In Telegram, create a bot with @BotFather. It gives you a bot token.
  2. Give NSKO QUANT the bot token (stored in your keychain vault) and your chat ID (the paired chat).
  3. Send /status to the bot to test. /kill stops everything.

Turn each channel on or off in Settings → Alerts (phone: ntfy, Telegram bot, desktop notifications).

If you cannot see a box for your topic or token

In this version the on/off switches are in Settings, and the ntfy topic and Telegram details are stored by the engine. If your Settings screen has no box to type them in, NSKO support will pair them with you. Never paste a bot token into a chat or email.

11 · Updating your strategy

Three ways to update

Every change makes a new version. Every change is recorded. You can always go back one version with one click.

Manual

Edit the rules in the Strategy Book (Edit rules), re-run the Lab, then promote it yourself.

Automatic

If the Lab finds a version that beats the current one on unseen data (Sharpe at least 0.2 higher), it goes to paper. Moving it to live is set to ask you by default.

NSKO strategy packs

Strategy packs signed by NSKO. The app checks the signature. Packs install into the Lab only, never straight to live.

Choose how updates work in Settings → Strategy updates: Manual, Automatic → paper, then ask me, or Ask me.

12 · How to withdraw your money

You withdraw. At your broker.

NSKO QUANT cannot move money. The Withdraw helper only works out how much is free and opens your broker's own withdrawal page. You log in there with your own password and 2FA.

  1. Pause new trades. Open trades keep their stops and targets at the broker. Nothing new opens.
  2. Close positions (optional). Only if you want to take out the money that open trades are using as margin. Tick "Yes, close all open positions", then Close positions. They close at market price.
  3. Withdraw at your broker. The helper shows Free to withdraw (equity minus margin in use). Press the button to open your broker's withdrawal page in your browser. Withdraw to your own bank there.
  4. Rescale and resume. Press I have withdrawn · rescale and resume. The engine re-reads your balance. New trades are sized from the new, smaller equity (1% of the new amount).
Good to know
  • Your broker may take a few days to pay out, and may charge a fee. That is between you and your broker.
  • If you take out a lot, your 1% per trade gets smaller in £. That is on purpose.
  • Never give anyone your broker password. NSKO will never ask for it.

FAQ

Does NSKO hold my money?No. It stays in your own broker account, in your name. NSKO QUANT holds a trade-only key and has no withdrawal code at all.
Can NSKO see my trades or keys?No. Everything lives on your computer. Your account is encrypted there. There is no NSKO login server.
Does it trade with the window closed?Yes. The engine runs in the background as long as the computer is on and online.
Will I make money?Nobody can promise that. Most retail accounts trading CFDs lose money. Past and simulated results, including paper, do not guarantee future results.
Can I stop everything at once?Yes: hold the kill switch for 1.5 s, use the tray, or send /kill on Telegram.
Is the data on screen real?In demo mode, no: it comes from a simulator, and every screen says DEMO DATA. Switch to the live engine in Settings → Data source.
Can I loosen the limits in live?Only up to the hard caps: 2% per trade, 5% daily, 10% weekly, 10 trades, 4 open, 4% per market. The gate cannot be switched off in live.
Can I go back to paper?Yes, any time.
Does it need the AI model?No. Without a local AI model, research and the trade debate use built-in rules.
How do I move to a new computer?Settings → Desk → Export my desk with a passphrase, then Restore on the new computer. Broker keys are not in the backup: connect them again.
