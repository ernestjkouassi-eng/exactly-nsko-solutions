Technology with purpose.

Three programme concepts, how each would be funded and run, and the open funding calls that fit. Nothing here is funded yet.

Clean water

Low-cost sensor kits that check water quality at the tap and report it to the people who run the supply.

Concept

Health diagnostics concept

A simple screening device and app for clinics with little equipment. A concept only, with no medical claims until it is tested and approved.

Concept

Climate and food resilience

Drones and ground sensors that help small farms see crop stress, water use and soil health early.

Concept

Grant structure (each programme)

  • Phase 1: feasibility and lab tests, 3 to 6 months.
  • Phase 2: pilot with a delivery partner.
  • Phase 3: scale with partners and local makers.
  • Governance: monthly reports, open budget lines, independent evaluation.

Still needed

  • Decision: community interest company (CIC) or charity for this work.
  • Named partners and letters of support.
  • Confirmed company number and registered office.
