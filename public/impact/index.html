Impact

Technology with purpose.

Three programmes we want to build with partners and grant funding. Each is a concept today. Here is how each would be funded and run.

Clean water

Low-cost sensor kits that check water quality at the tap and report it to the people who run the supply.

Phase 1
Feasibility and lab tests, 3 to 6 months
Phase 2
Field pilot with a delivery partner
Phase 3
Scale with the partner and local makers
We need
A water charity or utility partner, letters of support, match funding
Status
Programme concept. Not yet funded.

Health diagnostics concept

A simple screening device and app for clinics with little equipment. A concept only, with no medical claims until it is tested and approved.

Phase 1
Needs research with clinicians
Phase 2
Prototype and lab validation
Phase 3
Regulated trials with an NHS or university partner
We need
A clinical partner, an ethics approval route, research funding
Status
Programme concept. Not yet funded.

Climate and food resilience

Drones and ground sensors that help small farms see crop stress, water use and soil health early.

Phase 1
Sensor and drone feasibility
Phase 2
Season-long pilot with growers
Phase 3
Open data tools for co-ops
We need
Grower groups, a research partner, grant funding
Status
Programme concept. Not yet funded.
